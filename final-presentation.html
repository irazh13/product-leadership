The case

Five deliverables, one strategy

Each lab committed one decision. Together they are the bet, the sequence, the team that owns it, the money, and what you learned building it.

Module 1
Product strategy
Where you play and how you win

Fable grows with users aged 25–45 across the U.S. and U.K. beyond acute stress — evolving with their needs from immediate relief to lasting everyday wellbeing.

The hard no

No clinical care or therapy — the regulatory and liability exposure outweighs the strategic fit for Fable.

Objective and key results

Objective: Make Fable a lasting part of users’ everyday wellbeing beyond the acute phase

- KR1: Reduce post-90-day churn among highly engaged users by 20%.
- KR2: Increase the share of post-90-day users who engage with Fable at least 3 days per week from baseline to 40%
- KR3: Increase premium renewal among users reaching the post-acute phase from baseline to +15%

Module 2
Outcome roadmap
Now, next, and later

NOW
- Redesign onboarding to establish an evolving wellbeing journey from Day One
- Build habits that encourage recurring engagement and make Fable part of users’ regular routines
- Build in reminders to support consistent routines and reduce disengagement

NEXT
Test and scale the most effective retention drivers
- Personalize ongoing support based on user history and changing needs through AI
- Introduce progress sharing with friends to test social connection as a retention lever
- Create content for different stages, interests, and evolving wellbeing needs
- Improve app performance by reducing load time from 4.2s to under 2s
- Close accessibility gaps to make the core experience more inclusive

LATER
- Localize Fable into Spanish and Portuguese after validating the retention model in current markets
- Extend everyday wellbeing routines to wearables through Apple Watch integration
- Expand acute anxiety support after establishing a sustainable post-acute retention model

Trade-off memo

Pushed out: Spanish & Portuguese localization, Apple Watch integration, and Crisis Mode — deferred until the post-acute retention model is validated.
Cut: Therapist matching, Web Version, and Fable for Teams - they either conflict with Fable’s regulatory and liability constraints or do not directly address the post-acute retention problem.

Module 3
Team charter
What we own

Own the Fable Growth initiative end-to-end, from strategy and prioritization through delivery and measurement of post-acute retention.

How we decide

Product owns priorities and scope. Engineering owns technical implementation.

Initiatives must demonstrate a measurable contribution to post-acute retention or the agreed OKRs to enter the roadmap.
Cross-functional decisions are based on evidence, OKRs, impact, effort, and risk.
Unresolved conflicts are escalated with clear options and trade-offs.

Module 5
Financial model
The business case

STRATEGIC BET
Evolve Fable from acute stress relief into an ongoing wellbeing journey starting Day 1, extending engagement beyond 90 days and increasing Premium retention.
Value: Sustained engagement → Lower churn → Higher Premium renewal → Longer customer lifetime → Subscription growth

KEY ASSUMPTIONS
1. Users have an ongoing wellbeing need beyond acute stress.
2. Introducing evolving habits from Day 1 increases engagement beyond 90–120 days.
3. Users adopt new routines as their needs evolve.
4. Sustained engagement improves retention and Premium renewal.
5. The existing 14-person team can validate the model incrementally.

EXPECTED RETURN
Target outcomes:
- 20% reduction in post-90-day churn
- 40% engaging ≥3 days/week after Day 90
- 15% increase in Premium renewal

FINANCIAL RETURN:
Incremental contribution margin from retained Premium users − incremental initiative cost
Scale only if validated economics meet Fable’s LTV:CAC and payback requirements.

Kill criterion

Investment is released progressively — each phase must earn the next.

PHASE 1 · Journey Adoption · 4 weeks
No positive lift vs. current onboarding → Pause & revise

PHASE 2 · Habit Formation · 8 weeks
No progress toward 40% engaging ≥3 days/week → Stop expansion

PHASE 3 · Retention & Renewal · Day 90+
No progress toward 20% lower churn and 15% higher Premium renewal → Stop investment

FINANCIAL GATE:
No full-scale rollout unless validated economics meet Fable’s LTV:CAC and payback requirements.

Module 6

What I learned

Where the strategy was hardest to write, what you will keep doing, and the moment your thinking changed.

Friction

There was not enough data to quantify the financial outcome and validate the strategy with numbers. Key inputs - such as the current active user base, Premium pricing and conversion, churn, and customer lifetime value - were missing, making the business case more directional than financially grounded.

Key learnings

The biggest learning was putting structure and terminology around practices I had already been using in real product work. I especially valued the clear framework for metrics - moving from doing these things intuitively to applying them more consciously and systematically.

Aha moment

A successful product can create its own next strategic challenge. Fable solved its original problem, but that meant it had to evolve. I immediately connected this to my volunteer project: once you achieve the original goal, the next question becomes not “What more can we do?” but “What should we evolve into next?”
